    The road to platinum

    Play the story in order, clear the missable trophies as you reach them, then work the side content, the collectibles and the cleanup. The platinum unlocks when the other 54 are done.

    1. 1 · Story first — Chapters 1–4 and the final mission

      Play the main missions in order: Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 4, then the final mission. The named missions — Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory — each carry their own trophy, so complete every mission the story gives you.

      Missable warning: every story trophy can only be earned while playing its chapter or mission. The official descriptions give no replay information, so do each one when you reach it.

    2. 2 · Strangers & side missions

      Finish the stranger strands across Leonida — eight full strands for the silver, plus the final mission for each of Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone. Take the hunting job for Bret when it comes up, and beat Cal in a fishing challenge.

    3. 3 · Activities & challenges

      Try every activity type once, then dig into the grinds: 250 stunts, 50 challenges, 20 different race wins, gold medals in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, a Top Fun drone race win at all 14 locations, an arcade game, and meditation at all 18 spots.

    4. 4 · Collectibles & the map

      Explore all 52 areas of Leonida, complete 3 collectible card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, register 10 of them in the Scooter Bros app, and buy all 8 garage properties.

    5. 5 · Cleanup — heists, money & the odd ones

      Rob 5 bank vaults, an armored truck and one illegal operation per gang; lose a 4-star wanted level with your partner; rob 3 shops back-to-back without losing the heat; take down 5 enemies in one Combat Focus; launder $250,000 of Marked Bills; spend $100,000 in shops; and pick up the stragglers — the cocktail on Ocean Beach, the showroom smash, the bowling alley, the melee drive-by and the jewelry store job.

    6. 6 · Platinum

      When the tracker shows 54 of 55, the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — unlocks on its own. Check the 100% completion gold too: it overlaps with almost everything above.

    Missable trophies are marked missable in the checklist. Everything else can be finished after the story, in any order.
